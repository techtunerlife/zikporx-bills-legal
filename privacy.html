Privacy Policy

Zikporx Bills · Effective October 3, 2026

Zikporx Bills ("the app") helps you track bills, debts, income, accounts, CDs/IRAs, stocks and crypto. Your privacy matters: the app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no Zikporx server. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you enter (bills, debts, income, balances, holdings, notes) is stored only on your device, and in the app's widget data on your device. We have no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

  • The items you add to the app, saved locally with Apple's on-device storage.
  • A summary used by the home-screen widget, shared only between the app and its widget on your device.
  • Your settings, such as reminder preferences and whether App Lock is on.

If you delete the app, this data is removed from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, the app's data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Market prices (stocks and crypto)

To show current prices, the app sends the ticker symbols you track (for example "AAPL" or "BTC") to third-party public data services: Yahoo Finance for stocks and Coinbase for crypto. Only the symbols and the date needed for pricing are requested. Your quantities, purchase prices, balances, and other details are never sent. Those services receive your IP address like any internet request and handle it under their own privacy policies. Prices are informational and may be delayed or inaccurate.

Device features

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: if you enable App Lock, authentication is handled by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Camera / Photos: if you scan a bill, the image is read on your device using Apple's text recognition and is not uploaded.
  • Export: if you export a CSV, it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's security, including your passcode and the optional App Lock. Keep your device secure and up to date.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this page.
